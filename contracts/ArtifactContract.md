# Artifact Contract v3.0

Schemas in `contracts/schemas/` govern machine structure; templates in `contracts/templates/` govern presentation. An artifact has one canonical path, one status, stable identifiers and explicit lineage. Drafts never authorize execution. Generated representations are derivatives and their hashes belong in `framework.lock`.

A material change returns to the owning decision phase. Evidence, review and closeout remain separate artifacts. Secrets and unnecessary personal data are prohibited.
