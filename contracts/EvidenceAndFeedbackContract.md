# Evidence and Feedback Contract v3.0

Evidence uses `evidence-envelope.schema.yaml` and distinguishes direct confirmation, identity confirmation, report, inference, non-execution and blocking. Every claim records origin, authorization, scope, time, hashes, sanitization, result, limitation and references.

Findings and warnings share `finding.schema.yaml`; material findings cannot be hidden as warnings. Executor reports, contract assurance reviews, delivery assurance proves, and follow-up assurance reconfronts the original ledger.
