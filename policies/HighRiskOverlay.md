# High Risk Overlay v3.0

**Owner:** high-risk

Activate this overlay for production, sensitive data, critical security, infrastructure, migration, deletion, protected history, external writes with material impact, low reversibility or broad blast radius.

Require explicit environment and resources, state before, blast radius, dry-run or justified alternative, idempotency, backup when applicable, rollback or roll-forward, observability, stop criteria, evidence and specific human checkpoints. Planning, implementation, simulation and real operation remain distinct. High-risk real actions are supervised and never inherit authorization from an earlier phase.
